Rate Grid Reconciliation

Adopt a new rate grid without moving anyone's projections. For every project this solves a per-month reconciling discount so each frozen monthly billing figure holds exactly against the new rack rates — the project total is the sum of those held months. Floor breaches, uplifts, and unsolvable months are flagged only. Dry-run first; nothing is written until you commit.
