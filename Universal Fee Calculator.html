    How this works.   The rates catalog (universal-fee-calc/rates-catalog.js) is the production Macro 2024 Hourly Rate Grid — the single source of truth for titles and rates. Each title family's Col B rack rate is the Mid billable tier; High and Low are ±10% off Mid. Rates are anchored at catalog base year 2024 and escalation compounds forward to each project year. Rate Lock holds rates at the project start year; the would-be escalation is credited at the bottom of the schedule.

    Cost-rate floor.   Col E (PowerBI) — falling back to Col G (Paylocity) where blank — is the cost rate for each tier. The calculator compares every role's effective billed rate (escalated to start year, net of the client discount) against that floor; any role priced below it is flagged as potentially unprofitable but is allowed through — the schedule still exports. These cost rates also drive the later planned-vs-actual margin comparison against Paylocity.

    Staff title vs. project role.   Each line carries a staff title (the rate-grid level that sets the rate & floor) and a free-text project role. The grid's page-2 Title→Rate mapping (57 HR titles) is stored as catalog data for normalising arbitrary titles back to a billable level.

    Grid vs. contracted rates.   Each role defaults to Grid (tier rate − industry adjustment, escalated). Flip a role to Contracted to type a pre-negotiated client rate: it bypasses the industry adjustment, still escalates year-over-year, and the client discount still applies. The staff title/tier stays attached so the cost-floor advisory continues to work. Ingested proposals land as contracted at their observed rate.

    Reconcile to proposal.   When a record carries a proposal total, the Reconcile to proposal bar back-solves to it by adjusting only the client discount — staffing allocations and per-role rates are held to the proposal (they're real). If the target sits above what current rates can bill, it flags the gap to close by raising rates rather than touching allocations. The cost-floor advisory still fires on any role pushed below cost.

    Export.   Excel export produces three sheets — Setup & Summary, Phase Matrix, Monthly Schedule — with live formulas so procurement can interrogate every number.
